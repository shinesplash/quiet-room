Fan Quieter

Experimental: cancels the steady hum of a fan using only this phone's microphone and speaker.

How to use

  1. Put the phone on your pillow right next to your ear, bottom edge (the microphone) closest to your ear, screen up.
  2. Turn the fans on.
  3. Press START and then stay still. Everything else is automatic (about 20 seconds).
Only the steady hum can be cancelled, not the "whoosh" of moving air. The quiet spot is just a few centimetres around the microphone.
